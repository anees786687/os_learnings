The parent waits for its child
Then both go their ways

My child process ends
Its parent never calls wait
A zombie lingers

Two processes share
One page of memory mapped
No copies between

Bind, listen, accept
The server waits on its port
A client knocks once

Connection refused
No server listens on port
I knock on the void

Segmentation fault
I wrote past my array's end
Core dumped, lesson learned

The kernel decides
Which process runs next and when
Fairness, not favour

Morning compile runs
No warnings and no errors
Coffee has gone cold

Time wait holds the port
Sixty seconds of silence
Reuse address, please
